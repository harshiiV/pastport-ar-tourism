PASTPORT – AR Heritage Tourism App
An AR-based heritage tourism application designed to make monument visits more engaging and informative. Developed as a group project, the team focused on identifying gaps in traditional tourism engagement through visitor-behavior research, then translating those findings into functional requirements for an AR experience — including monument interaction, content narration, and gamification logic.
Team Project
This was built collaboratively as part of a college team project, combining AR development, backend integration, and UX research across team members.
